Privacy Policy — Sip & Steps

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sip & Steps application ("the App") handles data. The App is a personal automation tool that creates and publishes short drink-recipe videos to the Sip & Steps YouTube channel, which is owned and operated by the App's developer.

Who this App is for

The App is used solely by its developer to manage their own YouTube channel. It does not offer accounts, sign-in, or any service to third-party or external users.

What data the App accesses

Through Google's YouTube Data API, and only for the developer's own Google account, the App:

  • Uploads video files and their titles, descriptions, and tags to the Sip & Steps channel;
  • Reads basic information and public statistics of that channel's own videos.

Data from viewers

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information from YouTube viewers or from visitors to this website. This site serves only static pages and sets no cookies and no trackers.

Use of Google user data

The App's access to Google/YouTube data is used exclusively to operate the developer's own channel as described above. This data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data storage

OAuth access tokens for the developer's own account are stored privately on the developer's own machine and server solely to keep the App running. No viewer data is stored.

Data retention and removal

Because no third-party user data is collected, there is no such data to retain or delete. The developer may revoke the App's access at any time via their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sipandsteps4@gmail.com.
