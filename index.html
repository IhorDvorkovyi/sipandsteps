Sip & Steps 🍹

Step-by-step drink recipes — cocktails & non-alcoholic — as short motion graphics.

Sip & Steps is a small automation project that produces and publishes short recipe videos to its own YouTube channel. It uses Google's YouTube Data API to upload videos to the channel it owns.
